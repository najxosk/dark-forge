# Dark Forge: Blood & Iron

**Forge legendary weapons.**

A blacksmithing game of heat, timing & gems. Take commissions, heat the metal to the golden band, strike at the perfect moment, and set the exact gem your customer demands. A wrong or cracked gem and the weapon is rejected.

## ▶️ Play

Open [`index.html`](index.html) in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari). Works on desktop and mobile. No install, no account.

## ✨ Features

- **4 phases of forging** — from apprentice to master, unlockable in the Workshop
- **Commissions** — customers, weapons, quality stars and rewards
- **Gem requests & chains** — set the exact gem, complete quest chains for unique trophies
- **Perfect-strike system** — heat, center and timing must all align
- **Fruit test** — a Fruit-Ninja-style test of your forged blade
- **Reputation** with factions, higher pay per level
- **A real ending** — forge the king's order to finish the game
- **Full EN/SK**, sound design, vibration, mobile + desktop
